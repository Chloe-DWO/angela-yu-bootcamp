My Favorite K-dramas of all time


Here are my Top 3 Kdramas that I would recommend:

1. AOS (AKA Alchemist of Souls)

I have seen reviews of this drama multiple times but I decided to watch it fully this year. When I tell you that I was not disappointed, I mean it. This drama is a fantasy romance about a girl named Nak-su, who is a powerful sorceress. She falls in love with a guy named Jang Wook, who is a prince. The drama is full of action, romance, and SO MANY GREAT PLOT TWISTS! It also has a great cast. I would highly recommend it to anyone who loves fantasy dramas.

2. True Beauty

I first watched this drama in 2022 and I LOVED it. It is a romantic comedy about a girl name Lim Ju-kyung, who is bullied for her looks. She learns how to do makeup and becomes popular at school. She falls in love with a guy named Kim Su-ho, who is also popular at school. The drama is funny, heartwarming, and has a great and good-looking cast (Especially Cha-eun Woo >-< !!). I would highly recommend it to anyone who loves romantic comedies.

3. Extraordinary Attorney Woo

I first watched this drama in 2022 and it was an amazing show. It is a legal drama about a girl named Woo Young-woo, who is a lawyer with autism. She is very smart and has a great memory. She falls in love with a guy named Lee Jun-ho, who is also a lawyer. The drama is heartwarming, funny, and has a great cast. I would highly recommend it to anyone who loves legal dramas or dramas about people with autism and is in for the ride.
