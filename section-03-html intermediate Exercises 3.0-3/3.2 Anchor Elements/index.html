My top 5 Favourite Websites

To be fair if I had to choose only 5 websites, these would be my top choices (as a CS student there are just too many to choose from):

  1. ChatGPT
    This is my No. 1 choice especially as a university student. I use ChatGPT for assistance with coding and research.
  2. YouTube
    I love watching videos on YouTube, especially tutorials and educational content.
  3. Pinterest
    Pinterest is my holy grail for finding and saving ideas, inspiration, and creative content. It was hard not to put it as my No.1 choice.😭
  4. TikTok
    TikTok is a great platform for discovering viral trends and creative content. I enjoy watching short videos and following my favorite creators. But tbh this take ALOT of my time 🤣.
  5. Instagram
    Instagram is a great platform for sharing photos and connecting with friends and followers. I don't use it much but it had to make it on the list because well...I just like it😂
